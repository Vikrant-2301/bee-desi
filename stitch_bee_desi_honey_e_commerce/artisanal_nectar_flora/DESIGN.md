## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the purity, heritage, and luxurious character of artisanal raw honey harvested from indigenous flora. It serves discerning culinary connoisseurs, mindful wellness seekers, and design-forward direct-to-consumer shoppers who view organic sustenance as both art and daily ritual.

The visual direction merges **Minimal Editorial Luxury** with **Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Generous, warm negative space evocative of sunlit apiaries and raw botanical canvas.
- Editorial typographic cadence: high-contrast, graceful serif display headers anchored by crisp, technical geometric sans-serif for commerce logic and specifications.
- Restrained frosted-glass accents that echo crystallizing raw amber, translucent honeycomb cells, and dew-dropped pollen.
- Tangible craft details: delicate micro-borders, organic earth tones, and warm, diffused golden ambient shadows.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic warmth with grounding earthen depth:

- **Primary (`#C17D2A`)**: Rich spun amber; evokes natural viscous clover and wildflower honey. Utilized for hero focal points, primary interactive states, key iconography, and selected badge borders.
- **Secondary (`#2B1A12`)**: Deep beeswax brown; derived from organic forest hives and toasted honeycomb crust. Used for high-contrast titles, navigation bars, solid action buttons, and dominant structure.
- **Tertiary (`#385637`)**: Deep botanical flora green; references wild forest flora, bee flora canopies, and certified purity seals.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6EE`)**: Raw unbleached parchment cream; establishes a warm, soft base that avoids the harsh clinical sterility of pure white.

### Hierarchy & Functional Rules
- Interactive primary CTAs transition from solid `#2B1A12` into a warm `#C17D2A` glow on hover.
- Glassmorphic certification tags utilize translucent layers of `glass-frost` bounded by delicate `glass-border` hairpins to simulate translucent honeycomb facets.
- Deep text elements leverage `propolis-charcoal` (`#1B1411`) against `honeycomb-cream` (`#FDF9F2`) backgrounds to guarantee WCAG AAA legibility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial, heritage grace of **EB Garamond** with the technical clarity and modern geometric stance of **DM Sans**.

- **EB Garamond**: Reserved for display, collection narrative hero titles, harvest provenance headlines, and product names. It conveys artisanal maturity, botanical antiquity, and high craftsmanship.
- **DM Sans**: Deployed for price points, ingredient breakdowns, harvest origin metadata, UI controls, navigation links, and long-form botanical reading. It prevents visual fatigue and secures crisp digital legibility.
- **Micro-labels & Badges**: Set in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) using `DM Sans` bold weights to give origin certifications and batch numbers an authentic botanical registry feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with high intentional whitespace, reflecting the quiet confidence of ultra-premium goods.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered canvas with `4rem` outer margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Product showcases leverage asymmetrical spans (e.g., 7 columns for jar staging, 5 columns for narrative tasting notes and purchase flows).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Critical actions remain sticky in bottom app-like viewports with thumb-friendly margins.

Sectional rhythm alternates between open airy story passages and compact, structured e-commerce product matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects generic synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Organic Warm Atmospheric Diffusion** and **Layered Translucency**:

1. **Ambient Warm Shadows**:
   - Elements rest atop diffuse, honey-tinted ambiances: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(43, 26, 18, 0.08), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(193, 125, 42, 0.06)`.
   - Floating elements (e.g., sticky cart drawers, quick-view flyouts) receive: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(33, 20, 14, 0.16)`.

2. **Glassmorphic Certification Badges**:
   - Designed to mimic translucent amber drops and honeycomb window cells.
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` with backdrop filter `blur(12px) saturate(140%)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(193, 125, 42, 0.2)`.

3. **Tonal Elevation via Surface Containers**:
   - Depth level 0: `#FAF6EE` (Base canvas)
   - Depth level 1: `#FFFFFF` (Product card tiles & purchase modules)
   - Depth level 2: `#F4ECE1` (Curated tasting note blocks & specs panel)

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances organic fluidity with modern architectural discipline. 

- Core interactive containers, cards, and input fields adhere to **Level 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px`), lending a polished, modern feel without sharp clinical edges.
- Floating micro-badges, pill badges for certifications (e.g., "Raw", "Single-Origin"), and numeric counter steppers use pill-shaped finishes (`9999px`) to echo droplet geometry.
- Large hero cards and packaging preview displays use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to soften visual boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid `#2B1A12` with text in `#FAF6EE`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle transition to `#C17D2A` background upon hover with an ambient golden glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #C17D2A`, typography `#2B1A12`. Hover triggers `#F4ECE1` fill.
- **Quick-Add CTA**: Circular or pill button featuring a minimal '+' icon, styled in frosted glass with a warm amber rim.

### Product Showcase Cards
- Encapsulated within crisp white or off-white `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(43, 26, 18, 0.06)`).
- Generous padding (`space-lg`), floating origin pill badge on top left, and high-resolution glass-jar imagery staged with warm ambient under-glows.
- Includes tasting profile indicators (e.g., Floral, Woody, Smoky) displayed via micro horizontal bars in `#C17D2A`.

### Purity & Certification Badges (Raw, Unfiltered, Single-Origin, Flora)
- Pill containers with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` background, and `1px solid rgba(193, 125, 42, 0.25)`.
- Label typography in `label-md` with `0.12em` letter spacing and an accompanying vector insignia (leaf, bee, drop).

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Clean cream backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #E4D8C5`, transitioning to `1px solid #C17D2A` on focus with no harsh blue outlines.
- Quantity selectors: Integrated pill units with minimal `+` and `-` actions in beeswax brown.

### Tasting Notes & Terroir Module
- Specialized horizontal card row showcasing moisture content, pollen grain count, seasonal bloom month, and GPS apiary origin coordinate labels using monospaced or tracking-spaced `DM Sans`.
