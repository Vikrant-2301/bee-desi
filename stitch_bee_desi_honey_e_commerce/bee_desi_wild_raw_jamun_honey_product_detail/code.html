Origin & Custodians

The Ancient Jamun Groves of Balaghat

Nestled in the lush riverine buffer belt bordering the Kanha tiger reserve, Balaghat houses India’s oldest wild groves of Syzygium cumini (Indian Blackberry / Jamun). During late May, dense creamy-white blossom crowns blanket the forest canopy before the onset of the monsoon.

We partner exclusively with indigenous Baiga and Gond tribal beekeeping self-help groups. Practicing non-destructive comb harvest, only the surplus ripe honey-capping outer rings of Apis Cerana colonies are collected, leaving the brood comb entirely intact for the colony's seasonal nourishment.
